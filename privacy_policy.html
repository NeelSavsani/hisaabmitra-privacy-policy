Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how HisaabMitra (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) handles information when you use the app to manage your account and, as features become available, your personal finances.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, HisaabMitra may process:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, Firebase user ID, and account creation time when you register.
  • Authentication information: Firebase Authentication processes your email and password to sign you in. The app does not write your password to the Realtime Database.
  • Financial information: If and when financial tracking features are available, information you enter—such as income, expenses, amounts, descriptions, and categories—may be stored to provide those features.

The specific information processed depends on the features you use. HisaabMitra does not currently include advertising or group-sharing features.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate you and show your profile.
  • Provide app features and store information you choose to enter.
  • Maintain app security and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Respond to privacy or support requests.

3. Firebase Services

HisaabMitra uses Google Firebase Authentication for email and password sign-in and Firebase Realtime Database for profile information and app data. Information needed for these services may be processed or stored by Google under its terms and privacy practices.

For more information, visit Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Firebase processes information to provide authentication and database services. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or when necessary to protect users, the app, or legal rights.

5. Data Retention and Security

Information is retained while needed to provide the app and handle account or data deletion requests, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. We use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children’s Privacy

HisaabMitra is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information where legally required consent has not been obtained. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to the app.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, services, or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Account Deletion

An in-app account deletion option is not currently available. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting the developer by email.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to neelsavsani7@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line HisaabMitra Account Deletion Request.
  3. Send the request from the email address associated with your account, where possible.
  4. State that you want your HisaabMitra account and associated personal data deleted.
Request Account Deletion

What happens after a request?

We may ask you to verify account ownership. After verification, we will process the request and delete the Firebase Authentication account and associated profile information under our control, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Request Deletion of Personal Data

To request deletion of personal data without deleting your entire account, email neelsavsani7@gmail.com with the subject HisaabMitra Data Deletion Request. Describe the data you want deleted. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing the request.

Some information may not be deletable separately from the account. If that applies to your request, we will explain the available options.
